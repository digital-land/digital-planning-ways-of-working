title: Conventions
---

A recommended set of conventions we should all follow across the program. The reasoning of sticking to these conventions is so that:

- Moving resource around the programme is needed. For example to cover annual leave or respond to increase demands from deadlines
- We use methods proven to abide by departmental governance and GDS service standards

We welcome challenge on conventions as well as the suggestions of new ones.
